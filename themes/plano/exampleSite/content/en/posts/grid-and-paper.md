The page starts from paper, not from white. A slightly warm surface keeps long
reading sessions comfortable and makes the ink feel printed rather than
emitted.

## The grid is structure, not decoration

The grid runs behind the content at a fraction of the contrast the text uses.
It tells you where you are without asking to be looked at; the moment a
background pattern competes with a paragraph, it has failed.

## One accent, used sparingly

Everything that needs to draw the eye uses the same cobalt: links, the active
number, the rule under a heading. No second color is introduced to say
something the first one could say.

> A palette with two accents is a palette that has not decided.

That constraint is the whole theme. It is easy to obey and easy to break, which
is why the CSS keeps every color in one token file.
